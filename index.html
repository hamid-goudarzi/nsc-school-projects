About us

Civil work for schools across Metro Vancouver

Neil Smith Construction is a civil contractor based in Richmond, BC, with over 50 years of combined experience in underground utilities, earthworks and site servicing. School projects are a core part of our work: new additions, modular classrooms and the site services and frontage works that go with them.

We work for construction managers and prime contractors on everything from site preparation to full civil scopes. Our own crews and equipment handle excavation, utilities, concrete and paving.

Before we mobilize we review traffic control plans, local bylaws and Good Neighbour programs, and every day starts with a toolbox meeting. We are COR certified.

What we build

  • Sanitary and storm mains, manholes, detention tanks
  • Water mains and services
  • BC Hydro and communication conduits
  • Excavation, grading and shoring
  • Sidewalks, curbs, gutters, asphalt paving
  • Concrete pads and slabs
  • Demolition and site restoration

Our fleet

  • Excavators in various sizes
  • Loaders, backhoes, skid steers
  • Dozers and graders
  • Compactors
  • Dump trucks
  • Air track and coring drills
